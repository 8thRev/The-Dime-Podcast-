**Is This Platform the Amazon of Cannabinoids?**

Target carrying hemp beverages is a *major signal.* Not just because of its reach — but because of what it represents: **mainstream infrastructure validating cannabinoids.**

It's polarizing. It's strategic. And it adds another crucial data point that says the stigma is fading — and cannabinoid products, especially beverages, are *ready for the masses.*

But Target wasn't the first titan to test this space.

**Enter Edible Arrangements.**

Through their domain Edibles.com, the company quietly built one of the most intriguing cannabinoid access platforms in the country — one that blends logistics, brand curation, and scale.

At the center of it is **Thomas Winstanley**, whose deep background in cannabis brings credibility, restraint, and the operational trust needed to align with the industry's biggest brands — without losing integrity in the process.

Brands like **Wana, Wyld, Kiva, Cann, and Canntrip** now have a *secondary distribution channel* that connects them to a broader audience — one already trained to click *"add to cart."*

**Why This Matters**

Consumers can now **shop by experience, not just by brand.** They can choose *sleep, relaxation, or focus* the same way they shop for skincare or supplements.

That shift changes everything. It moves cannabis from **counterculture to lifestyle** — and positions edibles as the **gateway category** for new consumers entering the space.

Still, this evolution comes with risk. The legal frameworks are messy. The patchwork of laws can shift overnight.

That's why platforms like Edibles.com are built with scenario planning baked in — *"break glass in case of emergency"* pivots ready when regulations inevitably shift.

**The Hidden Advantage: Consumer Data**

Beyond distribution, there's a second-order effect — **consumer data**.

Yes, infrastructure and logistics are crucial. But the real value for the top brands joining Edibles.com is the ability to **get in front of new consumers** — especially those who've been curious about cannabinoids but haven't yet crossed the threshold.

Think of the *boomer generation*: They have spending power. They're intrigued. They're cautious — and brand loyal once converted.

That's the growth unlock.

Because if you can become a **boomer's first cannabinoid experience** — in *sleep*, *relaxation*, or *stress relief* — that's a lifetime customer.
