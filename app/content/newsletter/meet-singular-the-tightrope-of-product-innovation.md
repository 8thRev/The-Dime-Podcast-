Product Innovation in cannabis is like walking a tightrope.

Every element has to be perfect.

You need the infrastructure.

The manufacturing capability to keep costs controlled.

The flexibility to meet consumers where they are — and where they dare to go.

That balance requires deep knowledge.

Of the plant. Of extraction. Of how the industry works — and where it's heading.

Alex Kwon **does this better than anyone.**

Because true innovation demands foresight. You have to see every possible combination before it even exists.

**Meet Singular —** [**Active's**](https://active710.com/) **newest release.**

A culmination of all those lessons, limitations, and opportunities — built into one platform. Designed not just for today's market, but as the foundation for what comes next.

Testing, iterating, refining — endlessly. Every nuance, every component, every material decision has been challenged. From vision to engineering to manufacturing to design — nothing was left to chance.

The second-order effects? Simplified SKUs. Streamlined operations. And less chaos for large teams managing complex product portfolios.

These second-order effects may not make the bold headline — but they're the kind of innovation that quietly changes everything.

It's the same approach we've taken at [**Newton Insights**](https://www.newton-insights.com/) — replacing endless Google Sheets with a clean, digital platform that's easy to use and no longer controlled by just one person on the team.

Finding answers to why your yield changed is easy when everything is captured. The answers are no longer hidden.

This is what progress looks like. Relentless iteration. Clean systems. And a product built not for hype — but for endurance.

The truth is simple: what got you here won't get you to the next marker. Change is inevitable.
