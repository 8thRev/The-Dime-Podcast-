This week's episode is all about the factors that influence yield with **Dr. Greenhouse (aka Nadia Sabeh).**

Dr. Greenhouse sits at the intersection of **plant science, engineering, and HVAC** — a rare overlap that gives her a true *personal monopoly.*

*Personal Monopoly* is a term coined by David Perell to describe when three unrelated skills collide to make you the weird, irreplaceable expert.

In cannabis, these kinds of people are hard to find — but when you do, they're a goldmine of knowledge.

So I asked her: *When you show up onsite, what's in your toolkit? What are the absolute must-have tools every team should have?*

Here's what stuck with me:

- The balance of variables is harder than most want to admit. You can't optimize everything — you have to know which lever matters most for the outcome you want.
- Everyone has access to tech, but most don't have the right tools. Easy fix.
- There's one simple takeaway every operator can apply right now to improve their grow (we break it down in the episode).
